The work

Returns, books, and payroll — in a private office.

Tax preparation

I prepare personal and business returns — federal and California — and the 1099s and W-2s that go with them. We plan before year-end so the filing is not a surprise.

Accounting and bookkeeping

Monthly books, account reconciliation, and financial statements. You stay in control of the numbers; I handle the daily and monthly work.

Business management and payroll

Entity setup, transaction processing, and payroll. I sit with you on the decisions that change the return.

Information reporting

Estimated taxes, quarterly sales tax, and the filings your company has to send on time.
