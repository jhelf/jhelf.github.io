What we fix

Full-service plumbing for house and business.

Drain cleaning

We clear clogged sinks and toilets, backed-up main lines, and area drains with snake lines and hydro jetting.

Water heaters

We repair and replace regular and tankless water heaters so hot water comes back fast.

Leak detection and slab leaks

We find leaking fixtures and pipes, including slab leaks, and fix them cleanly.

Sewer inspection and repair

We run sewer video inspection and repair or clean sewer lines, including sewage ejection repair.

Toilets, faucets, and disposals

We repair toilets, faucets, and garbage disposals, and we install fixtures from Kohler, Toto, Moen, Grohe, and American Standard.

Gas, earthquake valves, and backflow

We handle gas leak detection, earthquake valve installation, and backflow testing and repairs, plus irrigation line repairs.
