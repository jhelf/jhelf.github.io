Who we are

A licensed crew led by the owner.

I completed a four-year California state-approved electrician apprenticeship with IBEW Local 11 in Los Angeles in 1974. I have owned Mountain Electric Company in La Crescenta since November 1976. We are five people. Every electrician on my crew completed a four-year state-approved apprenticeship.

We hold California license 326950 in C-10 Electrical and C-20 Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating and Air-Conditioning, and we carry an active contractor bond and workers compensation coverage. Service calls carry a $75 diagnostic fee, waived when you accept our estimate. Estimates for new work are $25. We take check, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.
