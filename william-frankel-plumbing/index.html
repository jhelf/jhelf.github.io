What we do

Drains first. Replacement only when it is earned.

Drain cleaning

We clear sewers, area drains, sinks, tubs, and toilets, including lines other shops walk away from.

Trenchless sewer replacement

When a sewer is truly done, we replace it without tearing up your whole yard.

Hydro jetting

High-pressure water scours grease and roots off the pipe wall so the line runs like it should.

Sewer camera inspection

We find the break with a camera and locate it from above, so digging starts in the right spot.

Water heaters and repairs

Faucets, sinks, disposals, water heaters, water lines, re-pipes, slab leaks, and filtration, repaired or replaced.

Gas lines and safety valves

Gas leak detection, pool and barbecue lines, fire rings, earthquake shutoff valves, and code compliance.

Who we are

Three generations of the same stubborn habit.

Bill Frankel started this shop in 1959 after moving to California from New York, where he had been a firefighter. When Bill retired in 1993, the Alexander family bought the business. We had already been doing all of the drain work and hydro-jetting for Frankel Plumbing for years, so nothing about the way we work changed.

We still go up on the roof to clear drains when that is what the line needs, and we still tell you when a cleaning will do instead of selling you a sewer. California licensed C-36, licence #270127.

Will you try to clear my drain before replacing it?

Yes. Clearing the line is always the first plan. Replacement only comes up when the pipe is truly finished.
