What we do

Tax and accounting help, under one roof.

Tax preparation

You do not have to be a tax expert. We prepare your individual or business return, check every deduction and credit, and answer all of your questions before it goes out.

Tax planning

Tax law changes every year. We plan ahead with you so estimated payments go out on time and April holds no surprises.

IRS problems and audit representation

Owe back taxes, missed filings, a lien, a levy, or a letter you are afraid to open. We negotiate with the IRS for you and represent you in audits.

Small business accounting

We take care of your books, payroll questions, and cash flow statements, so you can get back to running your business. Part-time CFO guidance when you need a professional financial manager.

Estate and financial planning

We walk you through estate planning step by step, and build a personal financial plan that manages risk and protects what you have built.

QuickBooks setup and training

New to QuickBooks or cleaning up a mess. We set it up, train you, tune it up, and answer your questions so your books stay in shape.
