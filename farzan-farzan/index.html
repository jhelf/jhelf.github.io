What we do.

Tax and accounting, handled by a CPA you can call.

Tax preparation

You bring the papers. We prepare and file your individual, business, trust, or estate return, and we check every deduction that applies to you.

Tax planning

You plan the year before it plans you. We look ahead at income, retirement, property, and payroll so April holds no surprises.

IRS problems

You got a letter and your stomach dropped. We answer audits, back taxes, liens, levies, garnishments, and payment plans, and we deal with the agency for you.

Small business accounting

You run the business. We keep the books, run payroll, form new entities, and compile or review your statements in plain language.

Trusts and estates

You want the paperwork right the first time. We prepare fiduciary returns and keep the accounting clean for trustees, executors, and their attorneys.

QuickBooks help

You want books you can trust. Aaron is a QuickBooks ProAdvisor. We set up your file, train your staff, and tune up the mess.

Who we are.

A father, two sons, and the firm they built.

Karl Farzan opened his CPA practice in 1986 to give people quality work at fair rates. His sons Josh and Aaron earned their CPA licenses and joined him, and together they formed Farzan & Farzan in Encino.

We are UCLA graduates in Business Economics and Accounting. Karl holds a Masters in Taxation and has worked in tax for roughly 30 years. Josh specializes in non-profit returns, trusts, and forensic accounting. Aaron passed the CPA exam at 20 and advises on retirement, property, and business deductions.

After ten years in Santa Monica, we moved to Ventura Boulevard in Encino. You reach us at (310) 394-0231 or info@farzancpa.com, and your first ten minutes on the phone are free.
