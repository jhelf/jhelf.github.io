What we do

Air conditioning, heat, and the work between.

Air conditioning

We install, repair, and maintain central air so a Granada Hills house stays cool when the valley hits triple digits.

Heating and furnaces

We service and replace forced-air furnaces and keep the heat coming on when January mornings go cold.

Heat pumps

We put in ducted heat pumps that cool in summer and heat in winter, including the permits and HERS testing the job needs.

Ductless mini-splits

We design and install mini-splits for rooms, studios, offices, and additions that never had ductwork.

Whole-house fans

We install QuietCool whole-house and attic fans that pull evening air through the house and give the air conditioner a rest.

Ductwork

We run new ducts, add returns, and fix the rooms that never got air, including vaulted west-facing living rooms that used to bake.
