Who we are

A CPA since 1985, and a husband-and-wife team.

I have practiced public accounting since 1985. I graduated with highest honors in accounting and economics from Assumption College in the Philippines, studied accounting and taxation at UCLA, and hold both Philippine and United States CPA licenses. I established this firm in 1997 and incorporated it in 2005.

My husband Ruperto earned his law degree from Glendale University College of Law and has practiced for more than 20 years in estate planning, probate, trust administration, and business formation. Together we give you tax and legal counsel under one roof, at 815 Colorado Boulevard in Eagle Rock.

We are members of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and the California Society of Certified Public Accountants.
